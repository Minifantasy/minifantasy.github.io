Minifantasy

The largest top-down fantasy asset collection at 8x8 resolution.

Released in April 2020, Minifantasy has grown by a new asset pack every month since: tilesets for dungeons, caves, deserts, swamps and towns, fully animated characters and monsters, crafting, spells, mounts, icons, UI and much more. Every set is modular, so packs combine freely with each other.

  • 70+main asset packs
  • 10,000sof tiles
  • 1,000sof props
  • 100sof creatures
